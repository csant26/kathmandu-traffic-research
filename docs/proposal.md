## Working Title

Can Kathmandu's Road Infrastructure Meet Current Traffic Demand? An Operational Evaluation of the Koteshwor–Tinkune Corridor

## Research Question

- What is the operational performance of the Koteshwor–Tinkune corridor under existing traffic conditions?
- To what extent can traffic management strategies improve traffic flow without shifting congestion to adjacent intersections or road segments?

## Objectives

- To analyze the existing traffic control and traffic flow characteristics of the corridor.
- To develop a simulation model of the corridor using SUMO.
- To evaluate the operational performance of the corridor using traffic performance indicators.
- To assess alternative traffic management strategies and compare their effectiveness with the existing conditions.
- To determine whether proposed improvements reduce congestion without transferring bottlenecks to neighboring road sections.

## Study Area

- Focuses on the Koteshwor–Tinkune corridor in Kathmandu Valley, Nepal.
- Koteshwor junction acts as a central point for all three districts: Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, Lalitpur.
- Plus, it holds all the necessary components like: traffic signals, pedestrian walk, zebra crossing, reverse lanes etc. which makes it more representative of the Kathmandu's traffic scenario.
- Inclusion of Tinkune in the study is to ensure that the congestion isn't being transferred to other junctions.

## Methodology

- Obtain the road network from OpenStreetMap.
- Process and validate the network for SUMO.
- Collect or estimate traffic demand.
- Build and calibrate the SUMO simulation model.
- Simulate existing traffic conditions.
- Develop and test alternative traffic management strategies.
- Analyze performance using Python.
